# EVENT POOL DESIGN v1 — Project Unicorn

**Status:** Design document. Feeds two tasks: the event-writing task (content) and the event-engine rebuild (engine). Neither starts before this is approved.
**Author:** Brain team (Erdem + Claude), 2026-08-19.
**Sources:** CK3 Dev Diaries #30/#75 and CK3 wiki (event sets, on_actions, weighted pools); Emily Short — "Beyond Branching", "Storylets: You Want Them", "What does your narrative system need to do?" (storylet / quality-based narrative); our own audits (K2/K3/K4, `get_history()` zero readers, dead-choice sweep, 94% retention fires); the four sealed reference texts.

---

## 0. The one decision everything else follows from

An event pool in a sim is either **texture** (the world breathes; something happens now and then) or **spine** (the game's actual story is told through events). CK3's community complaint — "a random count undresses at the feast; I click through, I can't tie a narrative around it" — is texture mistaken for spine.

**Ours is spine.** The founder fantasy lives in the world that talks back to the mechanics. Therefore the unit of design is not the event but the **arc**: a sequence of connected nodes about one named thing (an account, a person, a rival, a mentor) that **remembers** what the player did earlier. Single events are nodes in arcs. We do not write 40 events; we write **9 arcs of 4–6 nodes**, plus a small texture pool.

This is also the content-side answer to the audit's engine finding: `_history` is written and never read. Arcs read it.

---

## 1. Storylet vocabulary (so writer and engineer mean the same thing)

A **node** = one event = one storylet:
- **prerequisites** — when it may fire (phase, world state, arc memory, slot availability)
- **content** — TR body + EN body, both native, both in the sealed voice
- **options** — 2–4; some may be **locked-visible** with a reason line
- **effects** — only from the effect vocabulary; each option's effects trace to a reader the player sees
- **memory write** — what this node records for later nodes (`arc.node.choice`)

An **arc** = ordered nodes on one subject, with **memory** flowing forward and **cross-reads** into other arcs.

Two **trigger kinds** (CK3's on_actions):
- **event-triggered** — something happened (ship, first revenue, churn, hire, rival move, promise T-3, bug threshold, phase gate). Deterministic, one admission path.
- **pulse-triggered** — the weekly pulse draws from the eligible pool by weight; weights vary by phase.

---

## 2. The player's hour — what the pool must do in each phase

Phase weights are not constants; they are the pool's dramaturgy.

| Phase | Emotional job | Density | Arcs active | Category weights (pulse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bootstrap** (min 0–20) | *"You are alone and the world doesn't know you."* | sparse | Frank (Y5), World texture (Y8), first-customer node of Y1 | Frank ×3 · World ×2 · Customer ×1 · Team 0 · Investor 0 |
| **Traction** (min 20–45) | *"The company needs you and you can't keep up."* — pressure accumulates; v2's casus belli piles up here | peak, 2–3 decisions/week | Promise (Y1), Fragile account (Y2), Rival (Y3), First hire (Y4), Product health (Y7), Frank nodes | Customer ×3 · Product ×2 · Team ×2 · Rival ×2 · World ×1 · Investor ×0.5 |
| **Series A Hunt** (min 45–60+) | *"The company grew; the decisions got heavier."* — fewer, bigger | 2/week, weightier | Investor (Y6), Team hardening (Y4 late), enterprise nodes of Y1/Y2, Frank closing | Investor ×3 · Team ×2 · Customer ×1.5 (enterprise only) · Rival ×1 · World ×1 |

**Rules of density:** one decision-class node per day max; no two nodes of the same category in the same week; mandatory beats (phase gates, endings, Frank seed) are exempt and always present. Weekly budget 2–3 (director ruling), calibrated at 3× speed.

## 3. The arcs — demo pool

Each arc: subject · nodes (trigger · prerequisites · options · effects · memory) · cross-reads · payoff. Effects use the engine's existing vocabulary names where they exist; **`[VOCAB?]`** marks an effect the writer must confirm exists or flag as missing (never invent).

### Y1 · The Promise (B2B, per-account) — 5 nodes
*Payoff: at renewal, a kept promise is worth money; a broken one costs it. Memory is the mechanic.*
1. **The Ask** — event-trig: account enters "wants feature" (mid/enterprise only — small accounts never demand features) · slot `{account}` · options: promise (T=deadline) / decline politely / decline coldly · effects: `promise_create` | `satisfaction −` | `satisfaction −−, trust −` · memory `Y1.1.choice`.
2. **T-3** — event-trig: promise deadline − 3 days, feature not shipped · **the mail node** (sealed text) · options: tell the truth / ask for time / don't reply · effects: `trust ±` `promise_extend [VOCAB?]` `promise_break_soft` · memory.
3. **Delivered** — event-trig: promise resolves kept · short, isimli · options: 1 (acknowledge) + optional "ask for a reference" (locked unless satisfaction ≥ X) · effects: `_recover` seam (already fixed) `referral_lead [VOCAB?]`.
4. **Broken** — event-trig: promise resolves broken · options: apologise with credit / hold firm · effects: `mrr −` `trust −` | `trust −−, churn_risk +` · memory.
5. **At Renewal** — event-trig: contract term end (Layer B contract object; until then, gated off) · reads `Y1.3/Y1.4` memory: text variant differs · options: price up / hold / discount (player lever, capped) · effects: contract update.

### Y2 · The Fragile Account (B2B) — 4 nodes
*Payoff: pressure resolves; a loss feels like a loss. The director's cap (2 offers) becomes narrative.*
1. **First contact** — event-trig: account enters risk (edge, hysteresis 21d) · options: discount (cap 1/2) / promise (→ Y1.1) / listen (visit; costs a day) / ignore · effects per vocabulary.
2. **Second contact** — event-trig: re-enters risk after hysteresis · **different tone**, reads Y2.1 choice · options: discount (cap 2/2, then locked "İki kez indirim verdin") / promise / escalate to rep (locked unless Müşteri Temsilcisi exists — reason line) / ignore.
3. **The Silence** — event-trig: third risk entry · no options (1 acknowledge): the account stops calling. Memory only.
4. **Goodbye** — event-trig: churn resolves · isimli, kısa, no options; effects already applied. If retained instead: **Stayed** variant (short, dry).

### Y3 · The Rival (product/world) — 5 nodes
*Payoff: v2 has a name as its reason.*
1. **Noticed** — pulse (Traction) · rival named for the first time in ticker context · 1 option (info).
2. **They shipped it first** — event-trig: rival ships a feature on your roadmap · **the sealed rival text** · options: pull forward / let go / call the customer · effects: `roadmap_priority [VOCAB?]` `promise_create` etc.
3. **Share moved** — event-trig: market share delta ≥ threshold · info + one option (respond in v2 / hold).
4. **Customer asks** — event-trig: an account references the rival (reads Y3.2) · options: reassure / concede / counter-offer.
5. **They answered** — event-trig: after your v2 ships, rival responds (pulse-drawn variant) · info.

### Y4 · The First Hire (team) — 6 nodes
*Payoff: losing or keeping a person, not a stat.*
1. **Frank's nudge** — (Y5.3, cross-listed) after seed.
2. **The search** — event-trig: search started · 1 option + tone.
3. **First friction** — pulse (Traction, employees ≥ 1) · small: overtime, pay, a comment · options: talk / let it pass · memory `Y4.3.choice`.
4. **The jacket** — event-trig: employee flight_risk edge · **sealed text** · options: talk today / wait / ask `{another_employee}` (locked unless employees ≥ 2 — reason line) · reads Y4.3 ("last month you talked about salary").
5. **Stay/Go** — event-trig: resolution · reads Y4.4 · options: counter-offer / let go / (locked) promote (unless role allows).
6. **After** — short: the desk. Variant text for stayed/left. Memory feeds later team nodes.

### Y5 · Frank (mentor) — 6 nodes, all event-triggered, all one-shot
*Payoff: a relationship; the game's voice.*
1. Intro (exists) · 2. Seed offer (exists; Variant A) · 3. Nudge (exists; sealed text) · 4. **After first churn** — "sayı olduğu gün ara beni" (sealed) · 5. **At the Series A threshold** — "bu adamı tanıyorum" (VC name, one line of judgment) · 6. **Verdict** — at ending, one line per ending type.

### Y6 · The Investor (finance) — 5 nodes, phase-gated
1. **Early interest** (Traction, too early — the right answer is no) · 2. **Conference** (world node, pulse; attend costs days) · 3. **Term sheet** (existing scene) · 4. **Negotiation** (existing table system) · 5. **Close / walk away** (ending link).

### Y7 · Product Health (product, "doorbells") — 3-node loop, variant text per repeat
1. **Bug threshold** — event-trig: live bugs cross N (edge) · options: sprint (time cost) / carry on / (locked) route to support (unless support staff) · 2. **Named complaint** — event-trig: an account's satisfaction falls due to bugs · isimli · 3. **Better / worse** — event-trig: after sprint window · info + memory. **Repeats allowed**, never with the same text; the loop is what makes Kararlılık speak.

### Y8 · World Pulse (texture) — pool of ~6, pulse-triggered
Sector news, a hype wave hint, a conference invite, a regulation note, a Meridyen (YC) piece, a founder-lunch invite. Mostly info + one small decision. **≤ 20% of weekly draws.** This is the only texture; everything else is spine.

### Y9 · B2C User Arc (replaces Y1/Y2 in B2C runs) — 5 nodes
1. **Launch** (ship moment) · 2. **First reviews** (event-trig: audience threshold edge) · 3. **Product Hunt** — decision node: join (cost) / don't; then **separate outcome node** driven by quality (director grammar) · 4. **Power user** (edge) — a name, a request · 5. **Viral / collapse** (edge on WOM curve; two variants).

**Node count:** ~40 (Y1 5 · Y2 4 · Y3 5 · Y4 6 · Y5 6 · Y6 5 · Y7 3 · Y8 6 · Y9 5) — matches the demo target. Retire all 16 legacy events; keep their **functions** (ship moment, first revenue, phase gates) as nodes above.

## 4. Cross-reads (why arcs beat lists)

- Y4 (employee leaves) → Y7 (sprint stalls; bug threshold sooner).
- Y3.2 (rival shipped X) → Y1.1 weight up for feature X; Y1.2 text variant "the rival has it".
- Y2 (account lost) → Y5.4 (Frank calls); Y3.4 (customer cites rival) → Y2 risk edge.
- Y7.2 (named complaint) → Y2.1 for that account.
Cross-reads are **conditions + memory**, nothing else. The engine must let content read them.

---

## 5. Engine requirements (input to the rebuild arc)

1. **One admission path.** Every node — event- or pulse-triggered — enters through the same gate that applies suppression (one_shot, cooldown, phase, budget). `enqueue()` bypass ends (K2/K4).
2. **Two trigger kinds** as first-class: `on_action` hooks for event-triggered nodes; a **weekly pulse** with phase-weighted draw for pulse-triggered.
3. **Content-readable memory:** `history_has(arc, node, choice)` in the condition vocabulary; per-arc memory flags written by nodes. `get_history()` gets its readers.
4. **Slots + weighted targeting:** `{account}` / `{employee}` / `{rival}` filled by weighted pick — most arc-history first (CK3 §4) — never uniform random. Unfillable slot ⇒ option hidden or node ineligible.
5. **Locked-visible options with reason** (exists; standardise the reason line).
6. **Budget governor:** ≤1 decision node/day, category diversity per week, weekly cap 2–3, mandatory beats exempt.
7. **Variant text** per node (2–3 bodies) so a repeat never reads twice the same.
8. **Effect vocabulary published** to the writer; missing effects flagged `[VOCAB?]`, never invented (K3).
9. **Dead-choice sweep re-runs** on the new pool before merge: every option → effect → reader.

## 6. Authoring law — with reasons

- **Every event is a node of an arc**; header carries arc + node id. *(Spine, not texture.)*
- **Every node binds to a name.** *(CK3's "random count" death.)*
- **Trigger = state change**, never a standing state. *(K4.)*
- **Effects only from the vocabulary**; missing ⇒ `[VOCAB?]`. *(K3.)*
- **An option either opens a door later or shows locked with a reason.** *(Short: consequence as new option, not branching.)*
- **Cost is a real resource** — time, morale, promise, cash, brand — and the result is a number the player can read on a screen. *(No "pay to delete bugs".)*
- **Voice:** TR = Edgü cut (observation, no verdict, the outside world speaks); EN = dry observational, clipped (one image, shortest last sentence); Frank = short big-brother in both, verdicts only in his mouth. **No hyphen-dashes. No tab names. No invented mystery** ("a paper was signed"). *(Four sealed reference texts.)*
- **Every category has at least one opportunity/income node.** *(Pool measured negative-skewed.)*
- **Phase-appropriateness in the header.** *(You cannot join Product Hunt weekly.)*
- **Both locales written natively**, never translated; EN drafts by the writer, editorial pass by us.

## 7. Node file format (one file per node, `docs/content/events_draft/<arc>/<arc>.<n>.md`)

```
id · arc · node · category · market (B2B/B2C/both)
trigger: event:<hook> | pulse
prerequisites: phase, thresholds (edge), slots required, memory reads
slots: {account}, {employee}, {rival}, {another_employee}(optional)
weight (pulse only) · one_shot / cooldown / variants
memory write: <arc>.<n>.choice
--- TR ---
body · options (each: label · effects[] · locked_unless + reason)
--- EN ---
body · options
--- INTENT --- (prose, for the reader): what each option is meant to do to the world
```
