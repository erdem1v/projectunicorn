Erdem decided on 2026-10-22: the whole pipeline stays AI, including Steam capsule, logo, music and text. No freelancer artist or composer ("satıp satmayacağı belli olmayan bir oyun için binlerce dolar… freelancer'la çalışamayız"). The target is that the game does not "scream AI": criticism may come as "game is good but I dislike that it's AI-made", never as "AI slop".

**Why:** two-person team, no budget for human art iterations; he believes backlash targets games that loudly look generative, low-scope or cloned.

**How to apply:** Never recommend hiring humans for assets as the fix. Frame measures as: one art direction, many candidates + hand cleanup of AI tells, capsule derived from the game's own world, blind "is this AI?" test, typographic logo, cleanup of AI-ish prose patterns in text, honest specific Steam disclosure, don't hide AI-written code.
