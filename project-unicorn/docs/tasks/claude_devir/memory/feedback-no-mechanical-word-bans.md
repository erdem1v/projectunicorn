Erdem, after the Y5 prose revision (2026-08-19): "niye kelimeleri banlıyorsun, doğru düzgün kullansan yeterdi." I had turned the director's self-check grep (ceket, kahve, kart, coat, pen …) into a blanket ban: scrubbed meta prose ("card"→"modal"), flagged the shipped title "Frank'in çeki", and wrote sterile bodies to dodge words.

**Why:** the sealed reference text itself uses a prop ("Ceket giymişti. Ceket giymez."). The fault in rev 1 was *staging* objects and gestures (camera direction, mood sentences), not the nouns. A grep list is a heuristic for catching performance, not a law about vocabulary.

**How to apply:** when style feedback names words, fix the usage, not the lexicon: a prop or gesture stated as a fact is fine; one performed for mood is not. Never "sanitize" meta prose, titles, or shipped keys to satisfy a grep. Keep the checks that measure structure (Frank ≤3 sentences, sealed byte-match, dashes) and drop word blacklists.
